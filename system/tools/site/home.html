Four rules do most of the work

The components look alike because they obey the same few rules, not because they share decoration. The brand book has the rest.

Depth is fill

White page, grey tray, white field. A surface tells its children what they sit on, so nothing needs an outline or a shadow.

One yellow

Yellow marks the single most important thing to do in view. Everything else is quiet, ghost or a link.

Selected is solid ink

The current tab, the chosen segment, a checked box, a switch that is on. Yellow never means selected.

{{themes}} themes, each chosen

Every theme has its own values, picked against its own page, and an accent of its own. Try them in the menu at the top, or make one of your own.
