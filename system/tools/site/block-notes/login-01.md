The default login page for a product where people sign in with an email and a password. The card carries the wordmark, which links home, and the "Forgot it?" link sits on the line of the password label. The six other accounts come after the main button, as short names two or three across, and the foot of the card links to the sign-up page.

Start here and reach for a sibling only when the page needs more: [a cover beside the form](#b-login-02) to show the product, [a link by email](#b-login-05) instead of a password, or [account buttons first](#b-login-06). The card is 27rem wide at most and fills the width of a phone. The email field has `autocomplete="username"` and the password `current-password`, so a password manager fills both. The eye button switches the password to plain text and back; it keeps the name "Show password", and `aria-pressed` says whether the password shows.
