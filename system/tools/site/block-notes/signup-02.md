A split-screen sign-up page: the form on the white half, under a bar with the wordmark, and a green cover in the other half. The cover holds a headline and three points, each a title with one line under it.

Choose it when the reasons to sign up should sit beside the form. [The sign-up card with a picture](#b-signup-04) puts a panel like it inside one card and keeps it on a phone; this page gives the cover half the screen and hides it at 56rem and below, where the form has the page to itself. Under 40rem the form also stays at the top, under the bar, rather than centred in the height. The cover is a labelled aside, and its points are a real list whose check icons are hidden from screen readers, so each point is read as its words alone.
