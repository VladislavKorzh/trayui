The side panel of a calendar or booking app: the month picks the day the page shows, and the lists below choose whose events appear. It is an `<aside>` named "Calendars" rather than navigation, since nothing in it is a link. The month is a [Calendar](#c-calendar) with one stop for Tab: the arrow keys move by a day or a week, each day is a button named by its full date, and the chosen day reports `aria-pressed`. Each folding group of checkboxes has `role="group"` and the name of its heading.

Choose it for a tool whose sidebar filters one view rather than moving between pages, such as a schedule of deliveries, rooms or shifts. It is 21rem wide, like the message column of [two sidebars](#b-sidebar-08). Below 56rem the menu button, "Open the calendars", opens it in a sheet like any sidebar, and the month stretches to fill the sheet's width.
