A checkout page that asks for everything on one screen: contact, delivery and payment, each a fieldset with its legend, beside a summary that holds the Pay button. The form follows its own choices. Picking the farm shop hides the address and the time slots and makes delivery free, paying at the door hides the card fields and turns the button into "Place the order", and on today's date the slot that has passed is turned off. Use it once the cart is settled, after the [cart](#b-checkout-01), and follow it with the [order placed](#b-checkout-03) page.

Every field carries its `autocomplete` token, from the email to the card's security code, so the browser can fill the form, and the card number groups itself in fours as it is typed. Below 40rem the paired fields stand in one column, and below 64rem the summary and its button move under the form.
