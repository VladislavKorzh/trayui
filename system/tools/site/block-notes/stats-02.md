A season report built around one chart. The ink card at the top holds four stat cards, two by two until the row has 40rem of room and four across after that, over a bar chart of each month. Under it, horizontal bars rank six sources, the farm's beds in the example, and three meters measure the season against its goals. The page head has a select for the year to compare with and the one primary button. Pick it when one series tells the story of the period; for figures that each stand alone, use the [stat cards with a period switch](#b-stats-01).

Each chart is a stop for Tab, read as one image named by its written summary. The arrow keys walk the monthly bars, and a "Show the figures" fold under that chart opens the same numbers as a table with scoped heads. Each goal is a meter with its own name, such as "Jars sold".
