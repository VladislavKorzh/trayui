A passwordless login page: one email field and a button that sends a sign-in link, the kind often called a magic link. The hint under the field says how long the link works, and `aria-describedby` ties it to the field, so a screen reader reads it with the field. Under the six other accounts, a full-width button offers a passkey.

Choose it when the product has no passwords; [the plain card](#b-login-01) keeps the password field and the link for a forgotten one. The form stands on the white page with no card around it, under a bar that holds the wordmark. From 40rem up it is centred in the height; on a phone it stays under the bar. The passkey button is a plain button with no behaviour attached, so your own code calls the browser's passkey prompt, and its name, "Continue with a passkey", holds the words it shows.
