The front page of a magazine, a company blog or a news section, where readers land from the main navigation. Under the display heading come topic tabs with their counts, links to the archive and the search, one featured story as a wide card, six more in a grid of three, numbered pages, and a newsletter signup at the end.

Of the three article lists, this is the one for browsing what is new: [the search](#b-articles-02) serves a reader who has words in mind, and [the archive](#b-articles-03) lists everything by date. Below 56rem the featured card puts its picture above its text and the grid falls to one column. Each card is a single link named by its title through `aria-labelledby`, so a screen reader's list of links reads as a list of headlines rather than of whole paragraphs.
