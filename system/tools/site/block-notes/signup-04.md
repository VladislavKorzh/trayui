On this sign-up page one wide card, up to 62rem across, holds the registration form on one side and, in the same tray, a yellow panel about the first day with the product. The card has no wordmark, so the heading names the product instead.

Where [the split page](#b-signup-02) lists what the account gets and drops its cover on a small screen, this card shows what to do next and keeps its panel: at 56rem and below the two parts stack, the form first and the steps under it. The panel is a `tray-island`, which keeps the light palette in every theme, so it stays yellow with dark text in the dark theme too. The steps are an ordered list, so a screen reader announces three items in order, and their icons are hidden from it. [The sign-in card with a picture](#b-login-04) is its pair, with a dark panel.
