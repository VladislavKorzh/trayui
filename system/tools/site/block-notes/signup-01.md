Only a work email and a password: a registration form in a card, and the usual start for a sign-up page. The link for people who already have an account sits under the heading, and the foot of the card carries a one-line note on the terms, so there is no box to tick.

Start here and pick a sibling when the page has more to say: [a cover with what the account gets](#b-signup-02), or [the account buttons first](#b-signup-05). The password field has `autocomplete="new-password"`, so a password manager can offer a strong password and save it, and `minlength="12"`, so the browser stops a shorter one before the form is sent. The hint "At least 12 characters" is tied to the field and read with it. The eye button shows what was typed, for a person checking a password they have just made up. The card is 27rem wide at most and fills the width of a phone.
