A table of the day's work split by status, for a dispatch or support queue. Tabs carry a count each, from All to Late and Delivered, and each tab shows its own table. The order number in every row is a button that opens a sheet at the side with the order's progress as a vertical stepper, the address, a phone link and one action for its state; a late order adds an alert at the top. Pick it when the next step depends on the status; for search and bulk work, use the [table with filters and bulk actions](#b-table-01).

The sheet is the browser's modal dialog, named by the order number: focus moves into it, Escape closes it, and focus goes back to the row's button. The tabs follow the arrow keys. On a narrow screen the strip of tabs scrolls sideways and fades at its end, each table scrolls inside its panel, and the sheet takes the whole width.
