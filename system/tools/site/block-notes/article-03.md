The bottom of a blog post or article page, where the reader decides what to do next. It takes up the text at its last two sections and goes on with a card about the author, links to the previous and the next article, a comment thread with a reply set inside the comment it answers, a comment form with a character count, and three related articles as cards.

It pairs with [the long read](#b-article-01) or [the article with key facts](#b-article-02): those two are about the top of the page and the reading, this one about what comes after. The text, the comments and the form keep to one column of at most 42rem. The name and email fields sit side by side from 40rem, and from 56rem the previous and next links share a row, as do the three related cards. Every comment is its own article element, named "Comment by" or "Reply by" and its author, and each Reply button says whom it answers.
