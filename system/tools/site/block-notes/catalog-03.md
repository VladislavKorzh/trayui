A shop's range page, the step before a product listing: four category tiles with a count on each, a table that compares the sizes on sale, a card on keeping the product once it is open, and the three products ordered most. Each tile is one link, so the whole card is the target. Pick it as the landing page of a shop with a handful of categories, and send people on to a [catalog with filters on top](#b-catalog-01) or one [with a filter column](#b-catalog-02) for the products themselves.

The comparison table is a real one, named by the heading of its card, with `scope` on the column and row heads and the figures aligned to the end, so the price of 10 g can be read down the sizes. Below 56rem the tiles and the cards stack in one column, and the table keeps its 36rem and scrolls sideways inside its card.
