With one header across the whole product, the sidebar under it keeps only navigation: no wordmark at its head, no account at its foot. It suits products where search and notifications matter as much as the menu, such as a store admin or an internal tool used all day. The header is sticky and takes the colour of its ground, and the sidebar starts under it and stays in view as the page scrolls.

Choose it over [the base sidebar](#b-sidebar-01) when the account and the search belong in one bar on every page rather than at the foot of the tray. On a narrow screen the header folds part by part: below 56rem the menu button appears before the wordmark, and below 48rem the breadcrumb drops out and the search keeps only its icon, named by its `aria-label`. The notifications button says its count in its name, "Notifications, 2 unread", and the yellow pip is hidden from screen readers, so the number is not read twice.
