Three columns for working through records one at a time: the main navigation at the start, the list in the middle, and a details panel for the chosen record at the end, here an order to pack. That panel is an `<aside>` named after its order, with the facts as a description list, the upcoming deliveries as links, and the page's one yellow button in its foot, since packing is what you do to that record.

Pick it over [two sidebars](#b-sidebar-08) when the second column describes one thing rather than listing many. Both trays are 17rem wide, so the middle gets what they leave; with `tray-app__side--late` on the end column and `tray-app__menu--late` on its button, the details wait in a sheet until the window reaches 80rem. Below 56rem both leave the page: the menu button opens the navigation from the start side, and the info button, "Open the details of order PL-20431", opens the details in a sheet from the end side.
