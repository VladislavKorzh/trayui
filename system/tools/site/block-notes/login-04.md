One wide card, up to 62rem across, holds this login page: the form on one side and, in the same tray, a dark panel about the product. In the example the panel is a short [activity feed](#c-activity-feed) of three steps, each with an icon and its time. The card has no wordmark, so the heading names the product instead.

Where [the split page](#b-login-02) hides its cover on a small screen, this card keeps its panel: at 56rem and below the two parts stack, the form first and the panel under it. Choose it when that story should reach phones too, or when the page should stay white around a single card. The steps are an ordered list, each time carries a `datetime` attribute, and the icons are hidden from screen readers, so each step is read as its sentence and its time. The panel is a labelled aside, outside the form.
