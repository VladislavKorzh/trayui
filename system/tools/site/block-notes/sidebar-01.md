A fit for an admin panel or a small SaaS product whose pages all sit one level deep. The first group has no heading and holds the pages people open every day; the labelled groups collect the rest, and their labels are plain text, not controls. The account at the foot is a line of text: swap in a [User Menu](#c-user-menu) when people need to sign out from there. Nothing inside scrolls on its own, so if the list outgrows the window the whole tray scrolls, foot and all.

Move on to [sections that fold](#b-sidebar-02) when the groups multiply, or to [a second level](#b-sidebar-03) when pages sit under pages. Below 56rem the sidebar leaves the page, and the menu button in the top bar opens this same navigation in a sheet from the start side, named "Main" after its `aria-label`; Escape, the close button or any link inside closes it. The current page carries `aria-current="page"`, which turns its row solid ink.
