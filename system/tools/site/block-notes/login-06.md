A social login page, for a product where most people already have an account elsewhere. Six buttons, one per line, each carry the whole sentence, such as "Continue with Google", and a quiet "Use your email" link under them leads to a page of its own. With no field to fill, the card is a section named by its heading rather than a form.

Pick it when the email route is the exception. [The plain card](#b-login-01) shows the email and password first and the accounts as a row of short names; this one turns that order round. The page behind "Use your email" can be that card or [the email-only sign-in](#b-login-05). Each button's visible text is its full name, so it needs no `aria-label`, and a person who says "Continue with Apple" to voice control reaches the button they see. The list keeps one button per line at every width.
