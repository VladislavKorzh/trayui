For a product that has grown many areas, where each person works in two or three of them. Every labelled section is a `<details>` element with its heading as the summary, so it folds with no script: Enter or Space opens and closes it, a screen reader hears whether it is expanded, and the chevron turns a quarter as it opens. The first group, with Home and Orders, has no heading and cannot fold, so the daily pages stay in reach.

Choose it over [the base sidebar](#b-sidebar-01) when people use a few sections and skip the rest; when items need pages of their own beneath them, see [items that fold](#b-sidebar-04). Write `open` on the section that holds the current page, or its highlighted row is hidden on load. Nothing remembers which sections were open from one page to the next, so store that yourself if it matters. In the phone sheet the same sidebar appears with its sections folded as they were.
