A login page on a grey ground: the form is a white panel, with the wordmark centred above it. The line on the terms and the privacy notice sits at the foot of the page, outside the form, while the link to create an account stays at the foot of the panel.

Pick it over [the plain card](#b-login-01) when the page must carry that legal line, or when six account buttons would crowd the form: Google and Apple stay in view and a "More" button holds the other four. The three keep to one row at every width, and the menu opens upwards from the last cell. Enter, Space or the down arrow opens it on its first account, the arrow keys move through the list, and Escape closes it and puts focus back on "More". [The sign-up page on a tray](#b-signup-03) is its pair, for a product that wants both screens to match.
