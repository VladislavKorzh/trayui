An analytics dashboard of KPI cards, for a team that checks the same six figures every week. The cards fall into two groups, Sales and Packing and delivery, and tabs switch the period between 7 days, 30 days and the season; each panel says which dates it covers and what they are set against. Every card is one link to its report, with a trend and a spark line. Pick it when the figures matter more than their shape: the [season chart](#b-stats-02) adds one large chart, and the [breakdown by channel](#b-stats-03) splits a month into parts.

The colour of a trend follows `data-tone`, not its arrow: a pack time four minutes longer is marked bad, and the sign in the text, "+4 min", carries the direction for a screen reader, which skips the spark lines. Below 56rem the sidebar gives way to a menu button that opens it in a sheet.
