Six buttons make this social sign-up page, one per line, each with the whole sentence, such as "Continue with Google"; under them a quiet "Use your email" link leads to a page of its own. The line under the heading carries a short promise, here the length of the trial, and the foot holds the note on the terms. With no field to fill, the card is a section named by its heading rather than a form.
