Small products and full-width screens, such as a board or a map, can do without a sidebar. There is no app shell: the page is a plain screen with a bar along the top, and the Menu button at its end opens the links in a [Popover](#c-popover), as a hamburger menu does. Inside it the sidebar is `tray-sidebar--plain`, with no tray, padding or width of its own, so it takes the popover's surface and keeps the rows of the other sidebars.

Pick it over [the base sidebar](#b-sidebar-01) when the menu is opened now and then rather than kept in view; for a public site of a few pages, a [Navigation Menu](#c-navigation-menu) suits better. As the popover opens, focus moves to its first link; Escape closes it and puts focus back on the Menu button, and a press outside closes it too. It is the same popover at every width, with no sheet. The preview opens it with `data-open`; without it the menu starts closed.
