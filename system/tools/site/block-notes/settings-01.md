Account settings for a person's own profile, in four sections: the photo, the name and role, the contact details, and the language and time. Each section sets a title and a sentence of why beside a card of fields, and one bar at the end saves the whole form. Its note counts what differs from the saved profile, as in "1 unsaved change: the photo", and Discard puts every field back. Pick it for a form people fill in and then save; for switches that save one by one, see the [notifications by channel](#b-settings-02). Tabs under the heading lead to the three settings pages.

The note is a `role="status"` region, so each new count is read. The name, email and phone fields carry `autocomplete` tokens, and the note for the team counts its 160 characters. Below 56rem each section's title and sentence stand over its fields, as in every [settings section](#c-settings-section).
