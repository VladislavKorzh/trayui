A settings modal that opens over the page instead of replacing it: the product stays behind the dialog, and the dialog's own sidebar moves between its sections without leaving it. That sidebar is a second `<nav>`, named "Sections of the settings", inside a modal named "Settings", and the section shown carries `aria-current="page"`. Each switch in the pane is a checkbox with `role="switch"`, its title and a line of help inside the label.

Pick it when settings are visited briefly and people should land back where they were; for a settings area with pages of its own, see [profile settings](#b-settings-01). The block opens the dialog on load only to show it: in a product the "Open settings" button does that through the [Dialog](#c-dialog) script. Escape, either close button or a click on the veil closes it. Below 48rem the list of sections turns into one row above the switches that scrolls sideways, with its label hidden.
