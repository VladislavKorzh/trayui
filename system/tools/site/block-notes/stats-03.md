A breakdown of one month, for the question of where the orders came from and what they bought. A donut splits the orders by channel, a table of best sellers shows each product's share of sales as a meter in its row, horizontal bars rank the cities, and one large figure gives the share of repeat customers. A select picks the month, and the header runs across the top of the shell, over the sidebar. Pick it for parts of a whole; for figures over time, see the [stat cards with a period switch](#b-stats-01) or the [season chart](#b-stats-02).

The donut and the bars are stops for Tab, read by their written summary, and the table is named by its card's heading through `aria-labelledby`, with `scope` on every head. Below 48rem the breadcrumb leaves the header, and the table, at least 30rem wide, scrolls sideways inside its card.
