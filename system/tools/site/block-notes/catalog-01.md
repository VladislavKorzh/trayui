A product listing with filters for a small range: a breadcrumb, a heading with a line about the products, the bar, eight cards and a pager. Each card holds a picture, a name that links to the product page, a line of detail and the price, and its Add to cart button carries an `aria-label` with the product's name, so eight buttons with the same words stay apart for a screen reader. When people need checkboxes, a price limit and a stock switch at once, the [catalog with a filter column](#b-catalog-02) has room for them.

Each filter is a menu of checkable items that stays open while you tick. A ticked item becomes a chip such as "Type: Caviar", with "Clear all" beside it, and the row of chips hides while it is empty. The [filter bar](#c-filter-bar) fires `tray:change` with the filters that are on; the products that match are yours to fetch. The cards take as many 17rem columns as fit: one on a phone.
