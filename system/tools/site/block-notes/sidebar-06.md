In an app of wide tables and boards, a collapsible sidebar gives the width back. The panel button at the start of the top bar folds the tray to a rail 76px wide: each item shows its name in a [Tooltip](#c-tooltip) beside the icon, the workspace switcher and the account shrink to their avatars, and the usage meter steps out. A tooltip that only repeats its link's name is drawn for the eye alone, so it is not read twice; the Orders link has its own `aria-label`, "Orders, 12 to pack", which says what the bare count means.

Pick it over [the base sidebar](#b-sidebar-01) when screens need room more than labels. The button keeps `aria-expanded` in step and changes its name from "Collapse the sidebar" to "Expand the sidebar". Nothing remembers the rail between pages: the sidebar fires `tray:change` as it folds, so store the choice there. Below 56rem the button goes, and the sheet shows the labels even if the sidebar was a rail.
