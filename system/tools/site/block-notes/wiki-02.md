The front page of a knowledge base or a team handbook. An ink card at the top names the handbook, counts its pages, offers to start a new one and holds the search, with four frequent searches as links. Below it the sections are tiles, each with what it covers, its page count and when it last changed. Then come a feed of recent edits grouped by day, the five most read pages of the month, and the pages due for a review.

It is the way in to [the wiki page](#b-wiki-01) and [the page history](#b-wiki-03). Below 56rem the tiles and the three lists stack in one column, and the tree of pages opens from the menu button. The search is a `role="search"` landmark whose field has a visually hidden label, and "Most read" is an ordered list, since its order is the ranking, while "Due for a review" is a plain one.
