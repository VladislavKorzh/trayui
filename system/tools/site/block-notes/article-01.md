An article page for a long feature: a season's story, a case study, a guide that readers come back to. It opens with the headline, a standfirst, the author, the date and the reading time, then a picture across the whole width. Below the picture the text and its contents split into two columns, and the contents lists every section, with the sub-sections under them, as links to their headings.

Pick it over [the article with key facts](#b-article-02) when the piece is read from start to end rather than scanned for one answer, and put [the end of an article](#b-article-03) under it for the author, the comments and what to read next. From 56rem the contents takes a third of the width and stays in view while you scroll; on a narrower screen it comes first, above the opening paragraph. The headings step from h1 to h2 to h3 without a gap, the same two levels the contents shows.
