The ground of this sign-up page is grey, and the registration form is a white panel with the wordmark centred above it. The line on the terms and the privacy notice moves out of the form to the foot of the page, and the link to sign in sits under the heading, so the panel ends on the account buttons.

Choose it to match [the sign-in page on a tray](#b-login-03), or when six account buttons would crowd the form: Google and Apple stay in view and "More" holds the other four, in one row of three at every width. The behaviour gives "More" `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`, and its name, "More accounts to continue with", starts with the word it shows, so a person using voice control can say "More". The menu opens upwards, the arrow keys move through its accounts, and Escape puts focus back on the button.
