A security settings page with a danger zone at its foot. Two-step sign-in lists the authenticator app, the backup codes left and a switch for a code by text; the password card offers a passkey and sign-in alerts; and a list shows every device signed in, each with its own Sign out, plus one button for all the others. The last card hands the workspace to someone else or deletes it. Pick it for actions rather than fields: the [profile settings](#b-settings-01) are the page to fill in and save.

Delete opens an alert dialog that starts on the safe button and asks for the workspace's name. A wrong name marks the field invalid, says what to type and puts focus back in it, and a press on the veil does not close the dialog. Each Sign out has hidden words that name its device. On a phone the dialog's two buttons stack, each the full width.
