An archive page for a blog or a newsroom, for readers who remember roughly when something appeared. The years are tabs, each with its count. Within a year every month that has articles is a section with its own count, and each article is a row with its day, title, author, reading time and topic. A short line closes a year when its list needs a word, such as why the winter months are empty.

Use it beside [the front page](#b-articles-01), which shows the latest, and [the search](#b-articles-02), which finds by words. From 64rem the topics and the authors, each with a count, stand in a 20rem column that stays in view; on a narrower screen they follow the list. The years are a true set of tabs: the arrow keys move from year to year, and Home and End jump to the ends. The day in each row is a time element whose `datetime` holds the full date, so a bare "12" still carries its month and year.
