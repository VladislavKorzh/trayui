A shopping cart page that keeps the shop's header and footer around it. Each line has a square picture, a link back to the product, the price of one, a quantity stepper and a remove button with a tooltip. Under the lines, a meter counts towards free delivery, and three suggestions each add a line, or one more of it, then leave the list. The summary takes a promo code and ends in Check out. Pick it over the [cart as a checkout step](#b-checkout-01) to keep the shop's navigation, and over the [cart in a sheet](#b-shop-03) when the cart needs room.

Removing a line moves focus to the first line left, or to the empty state's link once nothing is left, and Check out turns off with an empty cart. The cart button in the header carries `aria-current="page"`, and its name follows the count: "Cart, 4 items". Below 64rem the summary moves under the lines.
