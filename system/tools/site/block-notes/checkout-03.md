An order confirmation page, for the moment after paying. It opens with a Paid badge, a thank-you headline and the order number with a button that copies it, beside the delivery window on the yellow card with a link to add it to a calendar. Under them come what happens next as a vertical stepper, the paid summary with the note to the courier, a card on where the product comes from, and an offer to keep the details by choosing a password. It ends the flow that the [cart](#b-checkout-01) and the [one-page checkout](#b-checkout-02) begin.

The copy button says "Copied", or "Copy failed", through a hidden status, so the result is heard as well as seen. Each finished step adds ", done" in hidden text, and the current one carries `aria-current="step"`. The password field asks the browser for a new password through its `autocomplete`. Below 56rem the six cards stack in one column.
