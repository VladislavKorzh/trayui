The home page of a small blog or a team's notes: one column of posts beside a sidebar. The newest post leads with a picture and a larger title; the five after it are cards of text with their date, reading time, author and two tags. Numbered pages close the list, and the sidebar holds an about card, the tags with their numbers of posts, and a form to get the posts by email.

This is the list readers start from; [the author page](#b-blog-02) and [the tag page](#b-blog-03) narrow it to one person or one subject. From 64rem the sidebar is a 20rem column that stays in view; on a narrower screen it moves under the posts and the pages. Each post is its own article element, and a visually hidden "Latest posts" heading sits above them, so the post titles are h3s under an h2 and the outline has no gap.
