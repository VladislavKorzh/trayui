An access denied screen for a product with roles: a packer opens the reports, which only owners and managers may see. A badge with a lock beside the title says who can. The figures and the chart stay on the page, dimmed, behind a prompt that names the owner who can open them and asks her with one button. Under it, a card lists what the person can do today instead.

Of the three empty states, this is the one where the data exists and the person may not see it: [First run](#b-empty-01) has no data yet, and [No results](#b-empty-02) hides it behind a filter. The prompt over the preview is the locked form of the [Upgrade Prompt](#c-upgrade-prompt). The dimmed copy is `inert` and `aria-hidden`, so neither Tab nor a screen reader reaches it. After asking, the button is disabled and reads "Request sent", a toast says the request went, and the note under it says when; focus moves onto the note as the button turns off, so it is not dropped to the top of the page.
