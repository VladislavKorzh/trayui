A tag or category page for a blog, for readers who follow one subject over the years. A display heading names the tag and says how many posts it holds and since when, and a strip of links switches to another tag. The posts are grouped by year in sections that fold, each with its count, and only the current year starts open. At the side, one large figure sums up the season and a short list names the people who write on the subject.

Pick it over [the blog home](#b-blog-01) when a subject has years of posts and the newest are not always the ones wanted; for one writer, take [the author page](#b-blog-02). From 64rem the side is a 20rem column that stays in view; below that it follows the years. Each year is the browser's own disclosure, so Tab reaches it and Enter or Space opens it, and its summary holds an h2 with the year and the count.
