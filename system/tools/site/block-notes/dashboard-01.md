An admin dashboard for the first screen after sign-in: the week at a glance, then the orders that wait. The page header holds the one yellow button, New order, with a quiet Export beside it. Each stat card compares the week with the one before, and a trend's colour follows its meaning, not its direction: the time to pack rises and is marked bad. The 7 and 14 days switch redraws the chart and its caption; the chart is a Tab stop named by a written summary, its figures in a table under "Show the figures".

The table filters as you type, sorts by any column and marks it with `aria-sort`, shows six rows a page, and wakes "Print the labels" once a row is selected. For more figures see [stat cards with a period switch](#b-stats-01), for a fuller table [filters and bulk actions](#b-table-01). On a narrow screen the sidebar moves into a sheet, the stat cards go two by two and the table scrolls sideways in its frame.
