A cart in a sheet, the slide-out cart of a shop that keeps people on the page they are browsing. Here it sits over a page of gifts with four product cards, and every Add to cart button opens the sheet and adds the product, or one more of it. The sheet lists the lines with a stepper and a remove button, then the meter to free delivery and the subtotal, and its Check out link repeats the amount. It has no promo code field and leaves the delivery to the checkout. Choose it over the [cart page](#b-shop-02) when shoppers add a thing or two and go on looking.

The sheet is the browser's modal dialog, named by its heading: focus moves into it, the page behind turns inert, and Escape closes it. Removing a line sends focus to the first line left, or to the close button. The subtotal is a polite live region. On a phone the sheet takes the whole width.
