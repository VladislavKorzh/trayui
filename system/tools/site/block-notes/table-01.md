A data table with filters, for work on many rows at once, such as stock that comes in batches. Above it sit a search, two filter menus whose choices become chips, and a date range with presets; under them, the count of rows selected and the bulk actions, with the destructive one kept in a menu. Each row shows what is left as a meter, a footer row totals the page, and numbered pages close the card. Pick it when people tick rows and act on them together; for looking up one record at a time, the [table with row menus](#b-table-02) is lighter.

The search hides the rows that do not contain its text, and an empty row says so when none is left. The head checkbox is announced as mixed while only some rows are chosen, and the count reads "3 of 10 selected". Collected is sorted on load, said with `aria-sort`. The table keeps 52rem and scrolls sideways in its card.
