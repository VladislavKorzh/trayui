A search results page for a blog, a journal or a help centre. The query stays in the field after the search, the topics are checkboxes in one segmented control, a year menu narrows by date, and each result gives its topic, date, title and one sentence with the matching words highlighted. Five results show at first and Load more adds the rest. At the foot, a row offers to write to a person when nothing found was of use.

Pick it when readers come with words rather than a date: [the archive](#b-articles-03) lists every article by month, and [the front page](#b-articles-01) shows what is new. The line with the count is a `role="status"` region, so "7 articles match" is read again whenever a topic or the year changes the list. Ticking no topic means every topic, and after Load more, focus moves to the first of the new results.
