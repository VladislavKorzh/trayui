A split-screen login page: the form on the white half, under a bar with the wordmark, and a yellow cover filling the other half. The cover puts a headline at the top and a proof from the product at the foot; the example shows the day's order count, a badge with the courier's time and a meter of the orders packed. The form is the one from [the plain card](#b-login-01), without the card around it.

Choose it when the sign-in page can show what the product does at a glance. [The wide card](#b-login-04) does the same inside one card; here each part takes half the screen. Below 56rem the cover is hidden and the form has the page to itself, and under 40rem it stays at the top, under the bar, rather than centred in the height. The meter carries `role="meter"`, a name and its value, so the packed count is read aloud and not only drawn.
