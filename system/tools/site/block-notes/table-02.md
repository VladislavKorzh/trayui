A data table of records to look up, sort and act on one by one, such as customers or accounts. Each row starts with an avatar and a name over an email, and ends in a menu to open the record, start an order, write an email or remove it, the last in the danger colour. Every column sorts, and Spent starts sorted from the most. The sidebar is folded to a rail of icons with tooltips, which leaves the table more width, and the pager reads "Page 1 of 53" between two arrows. Pick it over the [table with filters and bulk actions](#b-table-01) when nobody needs to select rows.

Each menu button is named for its row, as in "Actions for Bistro Kalyna", and the menus of the last four rows open upwards. In the rail every link keeps its name for a screen reader. Below 56rem the rail gives way to a menu button, and the sheet it opens shows the full labels.
