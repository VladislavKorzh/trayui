A sign-up page that turns the usual order round: the six accounts come first, then a rule that reads "or with your email", then the work email and the password. Like [the email-only sign-in](#b-login-05), it stands on the white page under a bar with the wordmark, with no card around it.

Pick it when many people sign up with an account they already have, yet the email form should not wait for a second page; [the account-only card](#b-signup-06) moves the form away, and [the plain card](#b-signup-01) puts the accounts after it. The group of account buttons is named by the visible words "Continue with" through `aria-labelledby`, not by a hidden `aria-label`, so a screen reader gives it the name on the screen. From 40rem up the form is centred in the height; on a phone it stays under the bar, at the top of the page.
