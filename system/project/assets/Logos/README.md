# Logos

The mark of Tray UI: a Tray Yellow tile with an ink bar set on it: a tray with one thing on it. It is the favicon of the site and stands before the name wherever the system names itself.

- `trayui.svg`: the mark, in Tray Yellow (`#ffd426`) and ink (`#141414`). It is drawn on a 32px box; the tile's corners are 10, the bar is 16 by 10 with corners of 4, and it sits 8 from each side and 8 from the foot.
- The name is set in type, not drawn: "Tray UI" in Onest at weight 700 with -0.04em tracking (the `tray-wordmark` class). The mark stands before it at about 1.25 times the size of the type, 10px away.

## Using it

- Keep the mark whole and upright, with clear space of a quarter of its width on every side. The smallest size is 16px.
- On a page in another theme the tile takes that theme's accent and the bar stays ink, so the page keeps one loud colour. Anywhere else the tile is Tray Yellow.
- The mark may move a little where it is a link: the bar is set down once when the page opens, lifts under the pointer and gives when pressed. Nothing else about it moves, and none of it moves for a visitor who asks for reduced motion.
- Do not outline the mark, put a shadow under it, tilt it, or set it on Tray Yellow, where the tile disappears.

This is the mark of the design system. A product built with the system brings its own.
