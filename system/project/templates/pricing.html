Questions about billing

Ask us the rest at billing@counter.example.

What happens when the 14 days end?

You choose Shop or Market, or the workspace moves to Stall, the free plan. Nothing is deleted, and we never charge a card you did not give us.

How does paying by the year work?

You pay for twelve months at once: $288 for Shop, which is $24 a month, or $792 for Market, which is $66 a month. Paid by the month they cost $29 and $79.

Can I change or cancel the plan?

Yes, at any time, on the Billing page. Moving up starts at once and costs only the difference for the days left. Moving down or cancelling takes effect when the paid period ends.

What if we take more orders than the plan has?

Orders keep coming in and none is held back. We write to the owner at 800 and at 1,000 orders on Shop, and at 40 and 50 on Stall. After two months over the limit we ask you to move up.

What does an extra person cost?

On Shop and Market, each person above the five or twenty in the plan costs $5 a month, or $4 a month when you pay by the year. Stall is for one person.

How can I pay?

By card, or by bank transfer when you pay by the year. Every payment has an invoice on the Billing page, with your tax number on it.
