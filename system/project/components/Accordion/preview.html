What does it taste like?

Mild and earthy, with mushroom and herbal notes, and less salty than sturgeon caviar. The eggs are firm and pop on the tongue.

How long does a jar keep?

Unopened, up to three months in the fridge. Once opened, eat it within three days.

How is it delivered?

By courier in a chilled box, the same day in the city, or you collect it at the farm shop.

Delivery

Courier, chilled box, 14:00 to 18:00.

Payment

Card, on delivery.
