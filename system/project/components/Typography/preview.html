Why we collect in the morning

A snail lays once it has dug in, and it should not be disturbed. So we leave the marked spots alone overnight and collect after sunrise. The weight goes into harvest.kg the same day.

  • Out and feeding: the bed is healthy.
  • Clustered on the boards: too dry, so we water before dawn.

It is slow work. One snail gives roughly 4 g of caviar in a year.Field notes, 12 June 2026
