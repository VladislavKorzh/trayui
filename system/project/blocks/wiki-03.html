 ## Grade the eggs
 
 1. Stack the 3.5 mm sieve on the 3 mm sieve, over the C tray.
 2. Tip the eggs of one collection onto the top sieve, no more than 500 g at a time.
-3. Shake the sieves for about a minute.
+3. Shake the sieves gently for 40 seconds. Longer shaking cracks the shells.
 4. Move what stays on the top sieve to the A tray, and what stays on the lower sieve to the B tray.
-5. Look over the A tray and take out any cracked eggs.
+5. Look over the A tray with the lamp and move any egg that is cracked, flat or grey to C.
 6. Weigh each tray without its lid and write the three weights in the batch book.
+
+> **Keep the eggs below 4 °C the whole time.**
+> Eggs that sit above 4 °C for more than 20 minutes go to grade C,
+> however good they look. If the door was left open, read the
+> thermometer before you go on.
