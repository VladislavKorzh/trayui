The snails have gone to sleep for the winter

Below 8 °C the snails seal their shells with a white lid and stop eating. We carried the last beds into the barn on Monday, and the farm stays quiet until March.

Why we collect the eggs by hand

A snail lays about 80 eggs at a time, a few centimetres down in the soil. Each clutch is lifted with a soft spoon, rinsed in cold water and counted before it goes to the cold room.
