# Code of conduct

Tray UI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to follow it.

In short:
- be kind, and assume good intent;
- give and take feedback on the work, not on the person;
- make room for people who are new;
- no harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, write to the maintainer privately, through the contact on the [GitHub profile of VladislavKorzh](https://github.com/VladislavKorzh). Every report is read and kept confidential. The maintainer decides on the response, from a word in private to a ban from the project, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
